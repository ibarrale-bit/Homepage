The Art of Youtuber Apologies

Youtuber Apologies are the thing that could make or break an influencer. As of the 2020s, have become their own subgenre of influencer content, given how prevalent Youtubers have become as entertainers in today's society. It's inevitable for content creators to become involved in some kind of controversy, backlash, or dispute. Since they're so heavily scrutinized by their audience, public apologies are required in order to save their reputation.

According to the Gettysburg College article, "Why Are Youtubers Bad at Apologizing?"Some big issues to when it comes to execution include.

  • Lack of acknowledgement of the wrongdoing
  • Lack of reparation/amends
  • Overdone "downcast" appearance (e.g. hoodie, lack of makeup, sorrowful expression and voice)

Here is a well-known example:

Colleen Ballinger

Youtuber Colleen Ballinger came under scrutiny in 2023 for unearthed conversations towards younger followers in the 2010s. Formerly known for her character Miranda Sings in 2008, many former fans spoke up about the inappropriate conversations and jokes Ballinger had exposed them to at the height of her fame.

Adam McIntyre is one notable person; originally, he had tried to share the accusations against her in 2020, only for Ballingers fans to cut him off. However, more fans began to come forward with experiences they shared with Ballinger that they felt were sexually exploitative. Despite Ballinger's team's advice not to respond to these accusations—in response, she posted the infamous apology video titled "hi." (aka "Toxic Gossip Train"), where the creator sings that strums a ukulele as she states "I didn't message them in a weird way, just a loser kind of way" and so on. It's to see how it was dubbed as "the worst apology video"; not only does she never show proof of accountability, but she tried to deflect the blame onto viewers for "spreading rumors for fun". It quickly soured the media's opinion of her, with further allegations being brought to light.

Ballinger continues to post on Youtube today, despite the fact that multiple other influencers/channels have cut ties, such as ZocDoc or Trisha Paytas.
